# Drafts

A draft keeps an automation, application, value, fix, or reference that isn't
finished yet, so you can come back to it later. A draft never runs, and
AppyTappy doesn't read it until you finish it and save it.

## Save a draft

While you make or change something, select **Save as draft**. Its **?** says
what a draft keeps. Until it can be saved, the editor lists what it still
needs, such as `Name the value.` or `Add an action.`

If you select another page while something isn't saved, AppyTappy asks
whether to leave. Select **Save as draft** there to keep your work, **Leave**
to lose it, or **Stay** to go back to it.

A value, fix, or reference draft keeps its screenshots and what you drew and
labeled on them. It doesn't keep a [recording](References.md#record-readings)
or the readings kept from one, so keep or finish those before you rely on the
draft.

## Finish or delete a draft

Drafts show on the page that lists what they make, with a **Draft** mark and
what each still needs. An automation or application draft shows under the
saved ones. A value, fix, or reference draft shows under its application.

- **Finish** opens it where you left off. A value, fix, or reference draft
  first asks for its application's window again, then brings back its
  screenshots. Save it as usual and the draft goes away.
- **Delete**, then **Delete draft** to confirm, removes it.

A saved item that has a draft of changes shows **Draft changes** and a
**Finish draft** button beside **Edit**. **Edit** still opens it as saved.

A draft whose application was removed, or whose value no longer exists, says
so and can only be deleted. So can a draft saved by another version of
AppyTappy Setup.
